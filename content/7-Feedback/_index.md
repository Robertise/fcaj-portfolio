> Here, you can freely share your personal opinions about your experience participating in the First Cloud AI Journey program. This will help the FCAJ team improve any shortcomings based on the following aspects:

### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working & Learning Environment**  
The First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) bootcamp provided an incredibly motivating, hands-on learning environment. Mentors and fellow cohort members were extremely supportive, sharing deep insights on AWS cloud architecture, serverless design patterns, and Generative AI best practices.

**2. Support from Mentor & Admin Team**  
Mentors provided continuous technical guidance throughout the design and deployment of **Pedix**. They encouraged self-driven problem solving - guiding me through AWS IAM permission boundaries, Bedrock inference quota management, and API Gateway VPC Link setup rather than just handing out answers.

**3. Relevance to Academic Major**  
As a Computer Science student majoring in Artificial Intelligence at Swinburne University of Technology, the bootcamp aligned perfectly with my career goals. It bridged the gap between academic Machine Learning theory and real-world Cloud AI engineering (RAG, Vector DBs, Model Serving, DevSecOps).

**4. Learning & Skill Development**  
I gained extensive hands-on expertise across 12 AWS Cloud Services (EC2, Bedrock, Qdrant, DynamoDB, API Gateway, CloudFront, Cognito, ALB, S3, CloudWatch, Lambda, Budgets), along with proficiency in FastAPI, PyTorch, React, Docker, and SSE Streaming architectures.

**5. Bootcamp Culture & Community Spirit**  
The AWS Study Group and FCAJ community fostered a culture of continuous learning, peer code reviews, and knowledge sharing.

---

### Key Takeaways & Recommendations

- **Most Satisfying Experience:** Successfully deploying the **Pedix** Level 4 Agentic RAG system live on AWS with full 5-stage reasoning trace streaming and cost optimization (~$72.7/month).
- **Recommendation:** I strongly recommend the First Cloud AI Journey bootcamp to any student aspiring to become a Cloud Solutions Architect or Cloud AI Engineer.
